# Proposal

## Why

Today a failed stage either loops automatically through a fixed, hardcoded path (`REVIEW`'s `CHANGES_REQUIRED` back to `IMPLEMENTATION`, `VERIFICATION`'s `FAILED` back to `IMPLEMENTATION`) or marks the workflow `FAILED` with no way forward (a technical stage-runner exception, a crashed `REVIEW`, a malformed verdict, or a verification check that failed to start at all). Plan 10 made terminal statuses (`COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED`, `FAILED`) immutable with no exit, which is correct for safety but leaves a workflow that hit a one-off technical glitch - a process crash, a timeout, malformed output - stuck in the same unrecoverable `FAILED` state as a genuinely exhausted workflow, with no distinction between "this needs a human decision" (today's only non-terminal pause, `AWAITING_USER`) and "this needs a retry." There is also no general, user-initiated way to retry a stage that failed for a technical reason without re-running every earlier stage, no bounded limit on how many times a correction loop or a technical retry can repeat before the orchestrator gives up, and no record distinguishing "the agent's run itself failed" from "the agent ran fine but the content needed another pass" in the retry history - `Workflow.outcomes()` is an undifferentiated append-only list today.

## What Changes

- Add an explicit `PAUSED` `WorkflowStatus`, distinct from the existing `AWAITING_USER`. `AWAITING_USER` SHALL continue to mean exactly what it means today: an ordinary gate waiting on a human decision (`VALIDATION_APPROVAL`, `ACCEPTANCE`, and the `NOT_CONFIGURED` verification pause). `PAUSED` SHALL mean a workflow stopped by a retryable technical failure with retry budget remaining. The two SHALL never be reported interchangeably. This reclassifies plan 13's `INFRASTRUCTURE_ERROR` verification pause: plan 13 originally routed it to `AWAITING_USER` alongside `NOT_CONFIGURED` (the only pause status available before this change); this change instead treats `INFRASTRUCTURE_ERROR` as a technical failure of `VERIFICATION`, pausing it at `PAUSED` under the technical-retry budget so a persistently broken verification environment reaches `FAILED` on exhaustion like any other technical failure, rather than sitting in an unbounded `AWAITING_USER` pause. `NOT_CONFIGURED`'s routing to `AWAITING_USER` is unchanged.
- Change the routing at the retry-budget boundary this change introduces: a technical failure of an eligible stage SHALL route to `PAUSED` (not directly to `FAILED`) as long as that stage-attempt-sequence's technical-retry budget has at least one attempt remaining. `FAILED` SHALL be reached only by **exhausting** a budget (technical-retry or content-correction) - the attempt that consumes the last remaining budget and still fails is what transitions the workflow to `FAILED`, not a later rejected retry call. `COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED`, and `FAILED` SHALL remain terminal and immutable exactly per plan 10's existing guarantee; this change introduces no exit from any of them, and `PAUSED` is explicitly non-terminal.
- Add a single, general, manually-invoked retry action (`retryStage`/`flow-retry`) usable while a workflow is `PAUSED` at an eligible stage, and while a workflow is `AWAITING_USER` at the `VERIFICATION` stage's `NOT_CONFIGURED` pause (reusing and generalizing the existing verification-retry/config-reload entry point rather than keeping a second, parallel command). Ordinary gate commands (`approve`, `reject`, `requestRevisions`, `requestFurtherWork`) are unchanged and SHALL continue to require `AWAITING_USER` at their specific gate stage.
- Classify every stage attempt as exactly one of: `INITIAL` (the stage's first-ever attempt), `TECHNICAL_RETRY` (the stage's prior execution did not complete validly - process crash, timeout, or malformed/unparseable output), or `CONTENT_CORRECTION` (the stage executed validly but its result required another pass - `REVIEW`'s `CHANGES_REQUIRED`, or a verification check that ran to completion and reported a failing result). A technical failure SHALL NOT be recorded or reported as a content correction and vice versa.
- Distinguish, specifically for the automated verification check, a **nonzero/failing exit code from a command that started and completed within its timeout** (a content correction - the check ran validly and found a problem) from a **failure to start the configured process at all, or a timeout before it completed** (a technical failure - the verification mechanism itself did not execute validly). Only the latter is eligible for a `PAUSED`/technical-retry pause; the former continues to loop to `IMPLEMENTATION` as a content correction.
- Preserve full attempt history: every attempt (initial, technical retry, or content correction) SHALL be recorded alongside the stage's prior outcomes, never overwritten or discarded, with its classification visible in the recorded history.
- Add configurable, bounded retry/correction budgets with explicit defaults, following the project's existing `@ConfigurationProperties` pattern: a maximum of **3** technical-failure retries per stage-attempt-sequence, and a maximum of **5** content-correction loops per workflow, by default. This replaces today's unconfigurable, undocumented `MAX_ADVANCE_STEPS = 50` loop guard with budgets that are explicit, configurable, and kind-differentiated.
- Scope retry eligibility by whether a stage writes to the target repository (or, for `CHANGE_PROPOSAL`, to the OpenSpec change's artifact files). Non-write, safe-to-re-run stages (`PROJECT_ANALYSIS`, `REVIEW`, `VERIFICATION`) are retry-eligible on a technical failure with budget remaining, with no special flag required beyond identifying the workflow. The write stages (`CHANGE_PROPOSAL` and `IMPLEMENTATION`) that fail technically SHALL still route to `PAUSED`/`FAILED` per the same budget rule, but a manual retry of either SHALL require the operator to pass an explicit `--acknowledge-partial-changes` flag on the retry command; a retry request for a write stage without that flag SHALL be rejected with the workflow unchanged. The orchestrator SHALL NOT, on its own initiative and without an explicit retry request, re-run a write stage as a technical-failure retry; this does not prohibit the orchestrator's existing, separate content-correction loop (e.g. `REVIEW`'s `CHANGES_REQUIRED` back to `IMPLEMENTATION`), which is normal control flow for a validly-executed stage, not a technical-failure retry.
- Auto-retry (unattended, orchestrator-initiated re-invocation of a failed stage) SHALL remain disabled by default, with no configuration path added by this change to enable it. The only ways a stage is re-run are: the pre-existing content-correction loop inside normal advancement (unchanged in kind, now explicitly bounded and classified), and the explicit, manually-invoked retry command.
- A `retryStage`/`flow-retry` call rejected for any reason (missing required acknowledgement, workflow not paused/not at the `NOT_CONFIGURED` pause, workflow terminal, stage not retry-eligible) SHALL consume no budget and leave the workflow's state, including both counters, unchanged.
- Add tests covering every edge case introduced above: a technical-failure retry succeeding on the next attempt; a technical-failure retry exhausting its budget and marking the workflow `FAILED`; a content-correction loop proceeding normally within budget; a content-correction loop exhausting its budget; a verifier process-start/timeout failure routing to `PAUSED` as a technical failure distinct from a nonzero-exit content correction; a manual `IMPLEMENTATION` retry being rejected without `--acknowledge-partial-changes` and accepted with it; retry being rejected for a terminal workflow; an invalid/rejected retry call consuming no budget; the `NOT_CONFIGURED` pause being retried through the same general command with no duplicate API surviving; and full attempt history being preserved and classifiable after multiple retries of each kind.
- Update documentation (`docs/workflow.md`, and wherever `pi-flow.verification.*`/`pi-flow.agent.*` configuration is documented) to describe `PAUSED`, the retry command, the acknowledgement flag, and both budgets with their defaults.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `controlled-stage-retry`: defines the `PAUSED` status, which stages are retry-eligible and under what condition (including the write-stage acknowledgement requirement, which covers both `CHANGE_PROPOSAL` and `IMPLEMENTATION`), the distinction between technical-failure retries and content-correction loops (including the verifier exit-code-vs-timeout split), the preserved attempt history, and the configurable, bounded retry/correction budgets with their defaults.

### Modified Capabilities
- `workflow-orchestration`: the "Correction loop" requirement's unconfigurable, undifferentiated loop guard is replaced by explicit, configurable, kind-differentiated budgets, preserved in full and extended additively rather than narrowed; a new requirement establishes `PAUSED` as a non-terminal status distinct from `AWAITING_USER`, with terminal-state immutability (from plan 10) restated as a boundary this change does not cross.

## Impact

- `src/main/java/.../workflow/api/WorkflowStatus.java`: gains `PAUSED`. **BREAKING** for any exhaustive `switch`/enum-set over `WorkflowStatus`.
- `src/main/java/.../workflow/api/StageOutcome.java`: gains an `AttemptKind attemptKind` field (`INITIAL`, `TECHNICAL_RETRY`, `CONTENT_CORRECTION`).
- `src/main/java/.../workflow/api/WorkflowEngine.java`: gains a general retry action (e.g. `retryStage(WorkflowId id, boolean acknowledgePartialChanges)` or equivalent), subsuming plan 13's verification-retry entry point so no duplicate retry method remains.
- `src/main/java/.../workflow/domain/Workflow.java`: gains `markPaused()`, a per-stage technical-retry counter, and a per-workflow content-correction counter.
- `src/main/java/.../workflow/application/WorkflowEngineImpl.java`: `advance()`'s `MAX_ADVANCE_STEPS` loop guard is replaced by the two new budget checks; the `VERIFICATION` case splits exit-code failures from process-start/timeout failures; a new retry entry point re-enters `advance()` for an eligible, paused (or `NOT_CONFIGURED`-paused), non-exhausted stage.
- New `RetryProperties` (`pi-flow.retry.*`, `@ConfigurationProperties`) with `maxTechnicalRetries` (default 3) and `maxContentCorrections` (default 5).
- `src/main/java/.../shell/FlowCommands.java`: gains a `flow-retry` command (with an `--acknowledge-partial-changes` flag) replacing any plan-13-added verification-specific retry command.
- Tests: `WorkflowEngineImplTest`, `WorkflowTest` gain cases for `PAUSED` transitions, eligibility, the acknowledgement flag, budget exhaustion, verifier failure-cause splitting, invalid-request budget non-consumption, and history preservation.
- Docs: `docs/workflow.md` and the configuration-properties documentation gain the `PAUSED` status, the retry command, and both budgets.
- Depends strictly on plans 10, 12, 13, and 14 being implemented first; this change consumes their landed shapes (`StageOutcome` fields, `VerificationResult`, change-binding) as fixed inputs and does not hedge against an alternate implementation order.
- No changes to `agent-invocation` capability or `PiRunner`/`PiRunResult` shapes.

## Non-goals

- Enabling automatic/unattended retry of any kind; no configuration toggle for this is added.
- Automatic rollback, resume-detection, or idempotency handling for a partially-written `IMPLEMENTATION` retry; the operator's `--acknowledge-partial-changes` acknowledgement is the entire safety mechanism for that one action.
- Reworking plan 13's verification configuration-reload mechanics; this change reuses that mechanism unchanged, only generalizing the command surface that triggers it.
